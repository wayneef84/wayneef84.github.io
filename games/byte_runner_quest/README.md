# Byte Runner Quest

## What is this?
Byte Runner Quest is the full game version of Byte Runner. The little courier robot gets 12 hand built levels across four worlds, a level select screen, golden disks to hunt, best times, and an Endless Run bonus mode. Where Byte Runner plays itself as a backup monitor, Quest is all yours.

**Who it is for:** anyone who likes short, tight retro platformer levels you can finish in a minute and replay for a better time.
**Why it exists:** Byte Runner's autoplay demo was fun enough that it deserved real levels.

---

## Quick Start (30 seconds)
1. Open **Byte Runner Quest** from the F.O.N.G. hub.
2. Press **Enter** or **Space** (or tap the screen) on the title.
3. Pick **1-1** and press **Enter**.
4. Run right, jump the gaps, stomp the bugs, reach the **GO** gate.

**Pro tip:** hold jump for a higher jump, tap it for a short hop. Bouncing off an enemy while holding jump launches you higher.

---

## Features
- **12 levels in 4 worlds**, each with its own look and a new idea:
  - **Dusk Circuit** (1-1 to 1-3): gaps, crates, one-way platforms, bugs.
  - **Glacier Grid** (2-1 to 2-3): slippery ice floors, moving platforms, vertical lifts.
  - **Ember Core** (3-1 to 3-3): lava pools, bobbing sparks, patrolling drones.
  - **Jade Garden** (4-1 to 4-3): springs, spike beds, everything mixed together.
- **Level select** with locks, best times and collected disks per level.
- **3 golden disks per level** (36 total), always in a spot that needs a bit of skill.
- **3 battery health** per attempt, a checkpoint in every level, instant out on pits and liquid.
- **Endless Run:** a random level that gets harder the further you go, with a best distance.
- **Saves automatically** in this browser (unlocks, best times, disks).
- **Keyboard and touch:** on-screen buttons on phones and tablets, tap the menus.
- **Chiptune sound effects**, M to mute.
- Every level and every disk is checked by a search bot to be reachable without taking damage.

## How to Play
| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Move | Arrows or A / D | Left and right buttons (or auto-run) |
| Jump (hold for higher) | Space, Z, K, Up or W | JUMP |
| Pause | P or Esc | II button |
| Settings | gear button | gear button |
| Turn around (auto-run) | Left or Right arrow | TURN button |
| Auto-run on or off | R | AUTO button in the top bar (rainbow border while on) |
| Mute | M | Pause menu |
| Menus | Arrows + Enter, Esc to go back | Tap |

**Rules**
- Reach the **GO** gate to clear a level. Your time counts from the start.
- Bugs and drones can be stomped from above. Sparks cannot; dodge them.
- Spikes and side hits cost one battery. Pits and liquid end the attempt at once.
- Losing all three batteries also ends the attempt. You restart from the last checkpoint flag you touched.
- Clearing a level unlocks the next one.

## Phone and tablet options
Tap the gear for one settings panel:
- **Fullscreen** (or the corner button). On iPhone it fills the browser window instead.
- **Rotate view** turns the game sideways when your phone is stuck in portrait.
- **Auto-run** with a direction: the hero runs by itself. Tap anywhere or press JUMP. TURN flips direction.
- **Left-handed** swaps the buttons.
- **Vibrate on death** (off by default, only where the phone supports it).
- **Max lives**: 1, 3, 5 or Unlimited (practice). Out of lives offers **Continue level** (replay this level X-Y from its start) or **Restart stage** (back to level X-1 of this stage), plus Level select. Lives refill and earlier progress is kept. The saved default is highlighted and starts after a 3, 2, 1 countdown. Tap the hint to wait, tap a choice to start now, tick "Always use this choice" to save it. Change it later under "When out of lives". Endless Run stays one life.
- **AUTO ON / OFF button** on the play screen switches auto-run at any time without pausing.

## URL parameters
- `?unlock` opens every level (handy for testing or showing off).
