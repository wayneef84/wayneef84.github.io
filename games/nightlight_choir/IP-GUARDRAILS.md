# IP guardrails (read before touching this game)

This game is inspired by the general idea of collecting creatures that each add a loop to a song. It must NOT copy My Singing Monsters (owned by Big Blue Bubble) or any other game. Game ideas and mechanics are free to use. Characters, names, art, music, logos and look and feel are not.

## Never
- Use any creature name, island name, rarity term, currency name, logo, color scheme or UI layout from My Singing Monsters. Examples to avoid (not exhaustive): "Singing Monsters", Mammott, Toe Jammer, Noggin, Potbelly, Furcorn, Quibble, Tweedle, Maw, Oaktopus, Drumpler, Entbrat, T-Rox, Thumpies, Wublin, Celestial, Shugabush, Plant Island, Cold Island, Air Island, Water Island, Earth Island, Fire Island, Ethereal Island, Diamonds, Relics, Starpower. Do not use the word "Monster" in creature names or the title.
- Open, scrape or copy from the My Singing Monsters wiki, fan wikis, breeding charts, stat tables or apps. Recipes, costs and numbers here are invented fresh.
- Copy art or audio from anywhere. No sound files and no images from the web, other games or AI sites. Art comes from `js/art.js`, sound from `js/synth.js`.
- Reuse the Classic pack from S4K Mixer v2 (images or loops). It comes from the community Sprunki game. Code from `synth.js` and `art.js` is ours and fine to reuse.
- Add accounts, analytics, ads, purchases, chat or any network call.

## Always
1. Log every asset and name in `ASSET_LOG.md`: what it is, how it was made (generated by our code, or written by hand), the date.
2. Check every new creature name and the game title with a quick web search for conflicts before it ships. Record the result in `ASSET_LOG.md`. If a name collides with a known game, character or trademark, rename it.
3. Keep our own twist on the mechanics: different worlds, different progression, our own recipes.
4. Keep it kid-safe: no scary content by default, no pressure mechanics (streaks, countdown shame, fear of missing out).
5. When unsure whether something is too close to another game, stop and ask Wayne.

## Ownership
The creatures, names, art specs, sounds and code in this folder are original work for this site. Keep the generator specs in the repo so the origin of every asset can be shown.
