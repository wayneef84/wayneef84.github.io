# Nightlite Choir+

A cozy music game for a young child. Little creatures each sing a short loop. Put them on a stage and the loops layer into a song. Three worlds: the Night Garden, the Toy Attic and the Star Observatory. All three doors are open.

Status: Milestone 2b (v0.3.0). The door screen opens all three worlds, 4 creatures each (12 in total).

## Play
Open `index.html` (works from file:// and GitHub Pages) or visit games/nightlite_choir/ on the site.
- Pick a door. The tray has three chips (Garden, Attic, Observatory). Tap a chip to see that world's 4 friends. All worlds share one stage, so friends from different worlds can sing together. The first friend placed sets the tempo. In the Observatory, soft star-map lines join the friends on the stage.
- Tap a creature to put it on the stage. Tap it again to send it home. You can also drag it to a spot.
- Undo, Reset, Mute, Volume, Save and Load (one mix).
- Keys: 1 to 4 place the friends shown in the tray, Z undo, R reset, M mute, S save, L load, Esc back to the doors.
- Home (top left) goes back to the arcade. Doors goes back to the door screen.
