# Nightlite Choir+

A cozy music game for a young child. Little creatures each sing a short loop. Put them on a stage and the loops layer into a song. Three worlds are planned: the Night Garden, the Toy Attic and the Star Observatory.

Status: Milestone 1 (v0.1.0). The door screen works and the Night Garden is playable with 4 creatures. The other two doors say "Coming soon".

## Play
Open `index.html` (works from file:// and GitHub Pages) or visit games/nightlite_choir/ on the site.
- Tap a creature to put it on the stage. Tap it again to send it home. You can also drag it to a spot.
- Undo, Reset, Mute, Volume, Save and Load (one mix).
- Keys: 1 to 4 place creatures, Z undo, R reset, M mute, S save, L load, Esc back to the doors.
- Home (top left) goes back to the arcade. Doors goes back to the door screen.
