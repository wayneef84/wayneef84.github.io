# 13 Card Poker - Manual

1. Pick how many NPCs (1 to 3) and the ante (1, 2 or 5 coins per point). Tap Deal.
2. Cards fly to every seat. Your 13 cards start high to low in the three rows.
3. Drag a card onto another card to swap them, or tap one card then another. Rows keep themselves sorted. Auto-arrange suggests a legal setup.
4. Rows must go Back >= Middle >= Front. The banner warns while it is a foul. Locking a foul needs a second tap and costs 6 points to each opponent.
5. Tap Lock. NPC rows flip one row at a time, back first. Green means the row won, red lost. Winning cards sweep toward the row winner, then coins fly from payers to payees.
6. The settlement panel lists points owed (loser to winner), each net result and the payments. Tap Next round.

Front hand ranks: three of a kind, then pair, then high card. A straight or flush in 3 cards counts as high card.

Balances show as "owes N" when below zero. Reset bankrolls puts everyone back to 100. Skip animations (or the arcade calm setting, or the device reduce-motion setting) shows final states at once.

NPCs (all legal, none foul):
- Dana is cautious. She picks the setup where the hand types of back, middle and front are closest together (smallest gap in hand category), then the strongest total.
- Rafi is greedy. He builds the strongest possible back row, then the strongest middle, then the front.
- Mina is balanced. She picks the highest weighted total, counting back x1.2, middle x1.1 and front x1.

Keyboard: Tab to a card, Enter or Space selects it, then Enter on another card swaps them. Focus stays on the moved card.
